01 / Understanding the brief

It started with a real workplace need.

The ESSP concept aims to simplify and centralize internal services for employees. Instead of switching between emails, manual processes and separate systems, employees need one mobile place to access services, submit requests and follow updates.

What the requirements meant for the design

The experience needed to make everyday services discoverable, keep frequently used actions within reach, and present useful information without overwhelming employees.

The supplied design shows a branded entry experience, employee home screen, service directory and additional people-focused screens.

02 / Framing the problem

Less searching.
More doing.

These are the design challenges implied by the project brief and the supplied screens—not claims from a completed user study.

01 / DISCOVERY

Where do I find it?

Employees should be able to locate common workplace services without navigating multiple tools.

02 / EFFICIENCY

How quickly can I act?

Important requests and shortcuts should be easy to recognize and reach from a mobile device.

03 / CLARITY

What happens next?

Information and request-related updates should feel understandable, predictable and consistent.

03 / My end-to-end design approach

How I take a requirement into a design.

This workflow explains how the ESSP requirements can be translated into a usable mobile experience. The supplied artifact confirms wireframe and final-screen visuals; research and testing steps are presented as the design approach rather than documented completed studies.

01

Understand the requirements

Clarify the employee tasks, business goals and expected features. Break the brief into key actions such as accessing services, viewing information and submitting requests.

02

Define users and pain points

Identify who uses the app, what they need most often, and where existing manual or fragmented processes create friction. Validate assumptions with employees and stakeholders when possible.

Map the experience

07 / Reflection and next steps

Design is more than the final screen.

The key design idea is to turn a broad set of employee services into a clearer, more connected mobile experience. The supplied visuals demonstrate the screen architecture and visual treatment, while real-world success would need to be evaluated through usability and product feedback.

What this design communicates

A recognizable identity, accessible entry points to services and consistent mobile patterns across the experience.

What I would validate next

Whether employees can find common services quickly, understand navigation labels and complete key tasks without unnecessary steps.
