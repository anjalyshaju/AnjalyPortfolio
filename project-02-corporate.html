01 / Overview

One portal.
Many everyday moments.

ESSP brings attendance, leave requests, workplace updates and social moments into one employee-facing platform. This case study focuses on the visual consistency and usability of two key surfaces: a white-label sign-in screen and a feature-rich daily dashboard.

THE EXPERIENCE

Personal → Actions → Social

Attendance calendar, summary chips and mood check-in sit alongside service shortcuts, celebrations, posts, events and announcements.

THE DESIGN GOAL

Clarity without losing character.

Preserve tenant-specific brand identity while making the interface readable, consistent and accessible across everyday employee tasks.

02 / The challenge

Three friction points.
One clearer direction.

The audit identified inconsistent surface brightness, action colors without a clear meaning, and a low-contrast attendance code.

01 / VISUAL CONTINUITY
+50 pt

Two systems, one product

The dark sign-in and bright dashboard had a large lightness jump without a consistent brand color carrying through.

02 / ACTION HIERARCHY
4 colors

Color without meaning

Four quick-action pills used different contextual colors even though they did not represent different states.

03 / READABILITY
1.25:1

An invisible code

The aqua COFF attendance label on white did not meet accessible text contrast requirements.

03 / The key improvement

A small color change.
A big difference.

The COFF code keeps its recognizable hue while shifting from aqua to deeper teal. Its letters remain visible, supporting recognition beyond color alone.

BEFORE · #00FFFF
30COFF
1.25:1 ✕ FAIL

Aqua on white. Below WCAG AA body-text contrast.

AFTER · #0B7285
30COFF
5.59:1 ✓ PASS

Teal on white. Above the 4.5:1 AA minimum for normal text.

Other audit recommendations: add a subtle border to pastel action tiles and use dark text on the amber quick-action pill.

04 / Final screens

Two surfaces.
One employee journey.

The visual language adapts to different contexts: a brand-led sign-in experience and a functional, welcoming dashboard.
